# Ten of Pentacles

Generations gather under the family crest, wealth passed through bloodlines.

## Upright Meaning
A legacy that outlives the body. Tokens and spirits for your side.

## Reversed Meaning
The inheritance is disputed. The ancestors show up for the other house.

*"True wealth is what we leave behind."*
