# Six of Swords

The ferry carries souls to calmer waters, leaving troubles behind.

## Upright Meaning
A planned crossing. Your units go home cheaper, ready for the next shore.

## Reversed Meaning
You refuse the boat, or the boat leaves without you. The opponent's units are the ones sent back.

*"Moving forward requires leaving something behind."*
