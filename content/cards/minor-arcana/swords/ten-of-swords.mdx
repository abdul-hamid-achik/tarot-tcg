# Ten of Swords

Ten blades in the back, the ultimate betrayal, but also the end of suffering.

## Upright Meaning
A final cut. You spend a unit or spend the opponent's hand.

## Reversed Meaning
The worst has already happened. Nothing else dies. Both sides draw and start again.
