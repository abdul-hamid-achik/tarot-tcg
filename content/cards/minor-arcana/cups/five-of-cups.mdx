# Five of Cups

Three cups spilled, two remain standing. Loss brings hidden opportunity.

## Upright Meaning
Grief first. You pay a price, then you learn from what is left.

## Reversed Meaning
You finally look at the two cups still standing. The loss is not denied, but it no longer runs the table.

*"From loss, we learn. From learning, we grow."*
