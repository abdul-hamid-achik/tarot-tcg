# Seven of Cups

Seven chalices float before you, each holding a different dream.

## Upright Meaning
Too many visions at once. Most of them vanish if you try to hold them all.

## Reversed Meaning
The fog lifts. One cup is real. You choose it and keep it.

*"Too many choices can be as paralyzing as none."*
