# Three of Cups

Friends gather in celebration, sharing joy and abundance.

## Upright Meaning
Company multiplies joy. The more allies you have, the more you draw.

## Reversed Meaning
The gathering empties. Isolation, gossip, or overindulgence. Everyone puts a card down.

*"Happiness shared is happiness multiplied."*
