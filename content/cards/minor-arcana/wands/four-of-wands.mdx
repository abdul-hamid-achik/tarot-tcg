# Four of Wands

A celebration of achievement, where success is shared with all.

## Upright Meaning
A homecoming. Your board grows. A full house draws extra.

## Reversed Meaning
The feast is postponed, or held at the other house. Their units get the toast.

*"In unity, we celebrate. In celebration, we unite."*
